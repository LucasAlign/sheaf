# Bridge domain language

## Authorized staff

A caseworker, county coordinator, assistant, or administrator whose organization membership grants access to the staff workspace. Bridge trusts content submitted by authorized staff and does not place their needs, posts, or photos into a second review queue.

## Published need

A need submitted by authorized staff. It is immediately available for volunteer matching and viewing until it is claimed, completed, expired, or otherwise no longer available.

## Completion confirmation

The one manual case-worker verification in the needs workflow. A volunteer may report delivery, but a need becomes completed only after staff confirms what was actually received.
